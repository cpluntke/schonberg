# Round 1: UX and musical review (singer + designer)

Reviewer persona: a singer in a young, high-level chamber choir (Poulenc, Debussy, Ravel, early Schoenberg) who reads solfège, with a section-mate who reads jianpu. Also a UX designer.
Build: dev server at http://localhost:5179, viewport 390×844, Chromium with a fake mic plus `?simulate=perfect|flat|sloppy`.
Scripts: `docs/qa/scripts/ux-tour.mjs`, `ux-play.mjs`, `ux-notation.mjs` and `ux-targets.mjs`. Screenshots are in `docs/qa/shots-ux/`.
Priorities follow `docs/priorities.md`.

## Verdict on the coaching progression

The ladder is the strongest part of the app. Listen → Learn → In time → Alone → Concert works like real part-learning: support comes away in the order a section leader would take it away. The sections are phrase-sized (4–9 bars), and *Next up* always gives one clear action. "Rehearsal-ready = L3 / concert-ready = L4" is stated on every piece page. Scoring passes and fails sensibly. A perfect singer passes at 100% (S). A singer 35 ¢ flat passes level 1 (±50 ¢) but is told "You tend to sing flat". A sloppy singer fails at 53–66%. Tenors are scored at sounding pitch, which is correct. Solfège and jianpu are spelled correctly for the key, including la-based minor and jianpu octave dots.

It is not yet a **cycle coach**, for two reasons:

1. **"By when" is missing.** The cycle's rehearsal and concert dates start as "–" and nothing asks for them. Nothing turns them into a pace either, such as "3 sections a week to be rehearsal-ready by 12 Nov".
2. **After a failed run the feedback is often unhelpful.** The results headline promises "tips below", and for a sloppy run no tips appear. The *Rhythm* % is really a second pitch score, so a singer with perfect timing is told their rhythm is 53%.

Live feedback is musically good: a pitch trace against the bars, ghost voices, and lyrics with the current syllable. Two problems make it harder to trust: the cents bubble jumps with normal vibrato, and the note-name gutter dims the wrong notes in sharp keys.

For real choir prep I would most want these, in order:

- a visual entry countdown and chord cue before mid-section entries
- dynamics and expression marks (already in the MusicXML, e.g. *cresc.*, *Plus lent*)
- a score or bar-context view
- a pacing plan tied to the concert date

| UX-01 | P1 | bug | Results promise coach tips that never appear | Debussy *Dieu!* S, bars 1–5, L1, `simulate=sloppy`. The sloppy singer sings about half the notes ~90 ¢ sharp, which is really a wrong note. `analyze()` counts these as "sung notes" (`|cents|<100`), so the median stays at +10 ¢ and no flat/sharp note fires. | UX-02 | P1 | bug | "Rhythm %" is pitch-gated, so it tells singers with good timing to fix their rhythm | `scoring.ts` records `onsetMs` only for samples **in tolerance**, and `rhythmValue(null)=0`. Every note sung on time but out of tune therefore scores 0 for rhythm. The sloppy simulator has perfect timing and still shows Rhythm 53% = Pitch 53% (`dieu-S-sloppy-results.png`). On Ravel bass sloppy it shows Rhythm 67% = Pitch 67%. Singers will drill rhythm they don't need. | Measure onset from the first **voiced, stable** sample (pitch within ±150 ¢ / any clarity > threshold), not from the first in-tune one. Report "—" for notes with no voicing at all. |
| UX-03 | P2 | bug | Note-name gutter treats accidentals as "chromatic" regardless of key; B and fixed-do "Si" are dimmed | `highway2d.ts` uses `diatonic = !/[♯♭#b]\|^(di\|ri\|fi\|si\|li\|…)$/i`. ### What's right (checked, no issue)

- **Voices and ranges.** SATB/SATBB names are correct. The tenor is scored and labelled at sounding pitch: G3 shows as jianpu "1" without dots in G, and F♯3 as "7" with a dot below (`nota-bachT-jianpu.png`). Yver's divisi basses are split into Bass 1 and Bass 2.
- **Spelling.** In B major (Debussy) D is spelled D (♭3 → "Me", jianpu ♭3), not C𝄪. Movable do is la-based: E minor gives E = La and D♯ = Si. Jianpu octave dots are correct above and below. Pitch classes use t/e.
- **Tempo and key.** These come from the files: Bach ♩=57, Bruckner 60, Debussy *Dieu* B major ♩=60, Yver 96, Ravel's tempo changes 60–90, Brahms 72. All are plausible.
- **Section lengths.** 5–10 bars per section, aligned with phrases in the pieces I checked. Ravel soprano correctly skips her tacet bars 14–19.
- **Level 4.** It hides note names and the guide, as specified (`l4-run.png`).
- **No console errors** in any run.
