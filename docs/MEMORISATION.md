# Learning a piece by heart

Memorisation is the last step of the ladder:

1. Note-learning
2. In time
3. Independent
4. Concert-ready
5. **Off book**

The tools below build up to it. Most make you produce the next bit from memory rather than look at it, because that's what makes it stick.

## Your map of the piece (Piece screen)

Every bar of your part appears as a small melody line. It is coloured by how it went recently, with newer runs counting more:

- blue: solid
- dark: ok
- orange: needs work
- grey: not sung yet

Marks show the kind of trouble: ♭♯ intonation, ⏱ timing, ↕ leaps, ✕ wrong notes. Tap a bar to loop it with its neighbours.

## Level 5: Off book

The other voices and the accompaniment play as usual. Only your part disappears:

- **Practise: fade out.** Bars you have sung well without note names and guide fade out. Hidden bars keep a rhythm line and the first letter of each word.
- **Test: all hidden.** Nothing of your part is shown. Notes appear only after you have sung them, as feedback.
- **Peek.** Hold it to see the next two bars for two seconds. A run with peeks is practice and doesn't count.
- **Memorised.** A section counts as memorised once you pass the test on **two different days**.

## Cold start (Piece screen → "Learning it by heart")

You're dropped into a random bar, hear two bars of the other voices, and come in from memory: no starting note, nothing shown. Bars you don't know yet come up more often. This trains the hardest part of singing from memory: finding your place again after a slip. Cold starts never change your levels.

## The words, on their own (Piece screen → "The words")

- **Words in rhythm:**
  - The music plays and you speak (or whisper) the text in time.
  - Only *when* each syllable starts is judged, from the loudness of your voice. Pitch never matters.
  - There are three steps per section: read along → first letters → from memory. 80% of syllables in time unlocks the next step.
  - The app hears syllables, not words, so pair this with the quiz.
- **Lyrics quiz:** no singing needed, a minute on the bus. Each question is one of:
  - the missing word;
  - which line comes next;
  - the line that matches its first letters.

  Lines you get wrong come back more often.

## Memory map (Piece screen)

A one-page outline of your part, per section. Read it (or print it) before sleeping:

- your entries, with the cue just before each ("a fifth up from the tenors' E");
- where you sing alone;
- where you have the tune;
- your range;
- the text in first letters.

## Practice beat (Settings)

Where nothing you can hear is playing (a solo, an exposed entry, a general pause), a soft click keeps the tempo. Choose "when I sing alone" (the default), "always" or "off". If you rush or drag in those passages, the coach tells you so.
